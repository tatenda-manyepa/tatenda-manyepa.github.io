03 / Experience

From the service desk to robots on the floor

Each role built a core skill of solutions work: understanding what customers need at Close Brothers, presenting and delivering solutions at Synamedia, and judging change against live operations at Inteq. Together, they’re what I now bring to solutions roles.

  1. 2024 – present

    Inteq

    Technical Engineer · Warehouse and robotics automation

    Supporting warehouse and robotics automation taught me to judge a change by how it affects live operations: orders shipped, carrier cut-offs met and robots back in service. I bring that same judgement to every solution I propose. What’s the right fix: a configuration change, a new integration, automation, AI or an engineering repair? What will it cost, and what value should it deliver? Who owns it, what evidence shows it worked and how does the team recover if it fails? I also helped shape ideas for internal AI tools to find technical information and investigate incidents.

    • Live operations
    • Warehouse systems (WMS / WCS)
    • Robot fleets (AMRs)
    • Incident investigation
    • Customer escalations

    Associate Technical Consultant

I helped configure and test broadcast platforms, supported a cloud migration and wrote Python scripts to automate repetitive engineering tasks. I learned to judge improvements by how well they fit the customer’s workflow and to test changes across the whole system.
