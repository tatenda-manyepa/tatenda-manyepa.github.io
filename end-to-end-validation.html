What We Are Aiming to Prove

The objective is not simply to show that individual AWS services or prototypes work. The final validation should demonstrate that the complete solution supports a representative operational workflow, remains observable and secure, handles failure predictably, keeps humans accountable for high-impact decisions and produces measurable operational value.

Technical Feasibility

The platform foundation, ECS-hosted services, managed data, messaging, integrations and AI components can work together without uncontrolled dependencies.

Operational Readiness

Support teams can detect issues, investigate evidence, follow runbooks, understand failure states and recover safely.

Security & Governance

Access, secrets, auditability, encryption and AI controls operate as part of the solution rather than as separate afterthoughts.

Business Value

The solution should reduce investigation effort, improve resilience and recovery, strengthen operational visibility and support safer AI adoption.

Validation Coverage

Each major architecture capability has a specific question that the final evidence must answer.

Area What We Validate Representative Evidence
AWS Foundation Network separation, controlled entry, workload placement and repeatable infrastructure. VPC/subnet layout, routing, security groups, Terraform output and deployment evidence.
Application Platform Containerised services run reliably across the intended target environment. ECS Fargate service health, task deployment, load-balancer checks and rollback evidence.
Data & Recovery Operational data is available, protected and recoverable without relying on manual failover assumptions. RDS connectivity, Multi-AZ configuration, backup policy, restore test and recovery notes.
Integration & Messaging Requests and events move between services predictably, including when dependencies fail. API test, Amazon MQ/event flow, retry or DLQ behaviour and failure-handling walkthrough.
Observability Teams can detect, understand and investigate abnormal conditions. CloudWatch logs, metrics, alarms, dashboard evidence and SNS notification.
Security & Audit Users and services have controlled access and important actions remain traceable. IAM boundaries, secrets handling, encryption configuration and audit evidence.
AI Knowledge Assistant Responses are relevant, grounded in approved knowledge and safe for decision support. Representative questions, retrieved sources, citations, grounded responses and feedback.
Human Accountability High-impact operational actions remain reviewed and controlled by people. Approval step, decision record, escalation path and human-validation evidence.

Representative Validation Scenarios

Scenario 1 — Normal Operational Flow

Question: Can a representative request move through the platform, data and integration layers successfully while producing the expected telemetry?

Scenario 2 — Messaging Failure

Question: If a message or consumer fails, is the failure detected, retained and diagnosable without silently losing the workflow?

Scenario 3 — Service or Dependency Failure

Question: Does the platform expose useful evidence, preserve service continuity where designed and support controlled recovery or rollback?

Scenario 4 — AI Investigation

Question: Can an engineer ask a representative operational question and receive a useful, cited and human-reviewable answer?

Scenario 5 — Access Control

Question: Can authorised users and services access what they need while inappropriate access is prevented and recorded?

Scenario 6 — Recovery Readiness

Question: Are backups, rollback conditions and recovery procedures documented and capable of being validated before production cutover?

Functional & Non-Functional Validation

Functional

Workflow completion, API behaviour, data access, message processing, retrieval, citation display, feedback capture and human-review steps.

Resilience

Service failure behaviour, Multi-AZ design assumptions, retry/DLQ behaviour, backup and restore readiness, and rollback paths.

Security

Least privilege, secrets protection, encryption, traceable access and separation between advisory AI and operational authority.

Observability

Logs, metrics, alarms, notifications and supporting context are sufficient to begin investigation without relying solely on manual discovery.

AI Quality

Groundedness, source relevance, citation accuracy, unsafe or unsupported responses, and user feedback on usefulness.

Operational Fit

Support process, runbooks, ownership, escalation routes and expected workload are understandable to the people who would operate the solution.

Architecture Decision

Wider rollout follows evidence, not assumption

The programme does not treat a successful technical demonstration as sufficient for enterprise rollout. Expansion follows only when the solution is useful, secure, supportable and proportionate in cost and complexity. Validation therefore combines technical testing, operational evidence, stakeholder feedback and business-value measurement before further investment is justified.

Success Criteria

End-to-end workflow completes

The representative journey crosses the intended platform, integration, data and AI layers without hidden manual dependencies.

Failures are visible and recoverable

Representative service, messaging or dependency failures produce usable evidence and follow defined recovery or rollback paths.

Security controls hold

Access is appropriately restricted, secrets are protected and important actions are auditable.

AI responses are grounded

Representative questions return relevant source material, traceable citations and guidance suitable for human review.

Operational users can support it

Monitoring, runbooks, ownership and escalation routes provide a credible operating model rather than a demonstration-only prototype.

Value can be measured

Validation captures measures such as investigation time, retrieval usefulness, failure detection and reduction in repetitive manual effort.
