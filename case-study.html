Executive Summary

Apex Operational Systems Ltd is a fictional UK enterprise operating a partially automated automotive-parts fulfilment platform spanning applications, databases, messaging, reporting and robotic fulfilment technologies. The platform supports critical inventory, order and fulfilment workflows but faces recurring outages, manual recovery, infrastructure constraints, limited observability and an immature disaster-recovery posture. This case study presents an AWS-first modernisation strategy designed to improve resilience, recovery, operational visibility and scalability while establishing a controlled foundation for AI-assisted operations.

Business & Architecture Drivers

The design begins with the operational risks and business outcomes that the architecture must address.

Operational Resilience

Reduce the business impact of component failures across critical inventory, order and fulfilment workflows.

Recovery & Continuity

Establish defined recovery objectives, validated backups, recovery procedures and tested failover capability.

Operational Visibility

Move from reactive troubleshooting toward centralised telemetry, proactive alerting and faster root-cause investigation.

Operational Efficiency

Reduce dependency on manual health checks, recovery procedures and repetitive incident investigation.

Scalability & Change

Support growth and peak demand while enabling safer, repeatable platform change and controlled evolution.

AI Readiness

Establish trusted operational data and knowledge foundations for AI-assisted retrieval, investigation and decision support.

Architecture Response

The target state is shaped by business requirements, controls and trade-offs. AWS is the selected implementation platform; individual technologies are chosen because they address a defined need.

Resilient Platform Foundation

Secure connectivity, workload separation, controlled access and resilient service entry points reduce avoidable failure risk.

Application & Integration Platform

Containerised services and controlled integrations create a more repeatable and evolvable application operating model.

Data, Availability & Recovery

Managed operational data, reporting separation, backup governance and tested recovery patterns strengthen continuity.

Security & Operational Visibility

Monitoring, searchable evidence, alerting, access controls, secrets protection and auditability are built into the design.

AI-Assisted Workflow

Trusted knowledge retrieval, guided investigation and human-reviewed recommendations introduce AI where it provides clear operational value.

Governance, Cost & Responsible Adoption

Cost control, accountable ownership, policy enforcement and responsible AI controls support sustainable adoption.

Expected Business Outcomes

Because this is a portfolio case study rather than a live customer deployment, these are the outcomes the programme is designed to achieve and validate.

Reduced Outage Impact

Improved service continuity and reduced disruption to critical fulfilment workflows.

Faster Recovery

Defined recovery procedures and validated backups supporting more predictable restoration.

Proactive Operations

Earlier detection of degradation through centralised monitoring, alerting and operational visibility.

Scalable Platform

Greater flexibility to respond to growth, peak demand and changing workload requirements.

Reduced Manual Effort

Less dependency on repetitive support activity and individual operational knowledge.

AI-Ready Operations

A governed foundation for trusted knowledge retrieval, guided investigation and future intelligent automation.
