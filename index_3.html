03 / Experience

From the service desk to robots on the floor

Each role added something a customer-facing engineer needs: listening, delivering, and judging change against live operations.

  1. 2024 – present

    Inteq

    Technical Engineer · Warehouse and robotics automation

    Supporting warehouse and robotics automation taught me to judge a change by how it affects live operations: orders shipped, carrier cut-offs met and robots back in service. I bring that same judgement to every solution I propose. Is AI the right tool, or will a rule or an engineering fix do? What will it cost, and what value should it deliver? Who owns it, what evidence shows it worked and how does the team recover if it fails? I also helped shape ideas for internal AI tools to find technical information and investigate incidents.
