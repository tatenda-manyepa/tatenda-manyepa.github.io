My AI Consulting Approach

A business-first consulting approach. The objective is not to introduce AI everywhere; it is to understand the problem, select the right intervention, validate the recommendation and measure whether it creates enough value to scale.

A fictional enterprise operations engagement showing how business pain points are assessed, classified into the right intervention, prioritised, designed and validated through focused proofs of value.

Business & Operational Context

Support teams are losing time to fragmented knowledge, manual investigation and repetitive administration while critical platform issues remain difficult to diagnose.

The environment supports fulfilment operations, application services, databases, messaging and robotic systems. The consulting objective is to reduce support friction and improve operational decision-making without treating every problem as an AI problem.

AI Suitability Assessment

Separate AI opportunities from deterministic automation and platform problems.

Knowledge retrieval and guided investigation are strong AI opportunities. Ticket-time capture and timesheets are better solved through workflow automation. Resilience, HA/DR and recurring infrastructure failures require platform engineering first.

Intervention types✨ AI-led🟡 AI-assisted⚙️ Automation☁️ Architecture-led👤 Human-led

Start with one high-value, manageable-risk Proof of Value.

The AI Operations Assistant is prioritised because it combines governed knowledge retrieval, guided investigations, log and code analysis and structured RCA support. Workflow automation for time capture and reporting follows as a lower-risk efficiency opportunity.

AI Suitability & Intervention Assessment

Not every operational problem requires AI. Each issue is assessed to determine whether the best response is AI, deterministic automation, architecture/platform engineering or a combination.

🟢 Strong AI opportunity🟡 AI-assisted⚙️ Automation-led☁️ Architecture-led🔴 AI not required
Business / operational problemAI suitabilityRecommended intervention
Engineers struggle to find system knowledge🟢 Strong AI opportunity✨ Governed RAG / enterprise knowledge retrieval across runbooks, known issues and technical documentation.
Complex incidents take too long to diagnose🟢 Strong AI opportunity✨ AI-assisted investigation using incident context, similar incidents, support knowledge and human review.
Large logs are difficult to interpret manually🟡 AI-assisted✨⚙️ Deterministic parsing for reliable extraction, then AI-assisted explanation, correlation and investigation guidance.
Unfamiliar code makes bug investigation difficult🟡 AI-assisted✨ Code & configuration analysis to explain behaviour, identify suspicious logic and suggest validation steps.
Engineers forget to record ticket time🔴 AI not required⚙️ Workflow automation to capture/prompt time recording from ticket activity, with user correction and approval.
Weekly timesheets require manual reconstruction🔴 AI not required⚙️ Deterministic aggregation of approved ticket activity into a reviewable weekly timesheet.
Operational stability / recurring outages🟡 AI-assisted☁️ Fix resilience and observability first; AI can then correlate alerts, logs and previous incidents to accelerate investigation.
Limited HA & DR🔴 Architecture-led☁️ Redundancy, automated failover, backup governance and tested recovery. AI is not the primary solution.
Reactive support model🟢 Strong AI opportunity✨ AI + automation for alert summarisation, similar-incident retrieval and recommended investigation steps.
Siloed teams & knowledge gaps🟢 Strong AI opportunity✨ Governed RAG alongside improved documentation ownership and knowledge-management practices.
On-premises infrastructure constraints🔴 Architecture-led☁️ Platform/cloud modernisation for resilience, capacity and scalability rather than treating AI as the solution.
Deployment & change risk🟡 AI-assisted⚙️ Strengthen CI/CD, testing, rollback and observability first; AI may assist change analysis and evidence summarisation.
Consulting decision: AI is prioritised where reasoning over unstructured knowledge, investigation context or technical evidence can create value. Rules-based work is automated conventionally, and underlying reliability problems remain architecture/platform responsibilities.

Solution & Technology Design

The solution is designed around the recommended workflows. Technology choices support the consulting recommendation rather than driving it.

AI OPERATIONS ASSISTANT

Knowledge & Process Query

Ask questions against approved system documents, runbooks, known issues and company processes using grounded retrieval and source references.

AI OPERATIONS ASSISTANT

Guided Investigation & RCA

Collect symptoms and evidence, recommend next checks, maintain an investigation timeline, suggest possible root cause and draft a structured RCA for engineer review.

AI + DETERMINISTIC LOGIC

Log Analysis

Parse application, WMS/WCS and robot logs deterministically, then use AI to correlate events, explain anomalies and propose investigation paths.

AI-ASSISTED

Code & Configuration Analysis

Explain unfamiliar code/configuration, highlight potential failure conditions and suggest evidence or tests without treating AI output as authoritative.

WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Ticket Time Capture

Track work against tickets, allow pause/resume and correction, and produce approved activity records without requiring AI.

AUTOMATION + ANALYTICS

Timesheets & Operational Reporting

Aggregate approved ticket activity into weekly timesheets and later reuse the structured data for operational workload and recurring-issue reporting.

Implementation direction: Python for application logic and APIs; Amazon Bedrock for governed model access; RAG/vector retrieval for enterprise knowledge; deterministic parsing for logs and time capture; Guardrails and human review for responsible use. The existing AWS target-state architecture remains supporting evidence for how the solution could operate securely at enterprise scale.

The implementation evidence focuses on validating the consulting recommendation rather than recreating the entire enterprise platform.

Building

Validate: usefulness, retrieval quality, groundedness, source references and investigation-time reduction.

Validate: correct parsing, evidence traceability, safe uncertainty and engineer usefulness.

Workflow Automation

Validate: time saved, correction rate, usability and data quality.
Govern

Responsible AI Controls

Approved sources, role-based access, citations, prompt/output logging, human review, escalation and cost monitoring.

Decision: AI supports operational judgement; it does not autonomously change production systems.
Measure

Evaluation Framework

Accuracy/groundedness, usefulness, latency, failure cases, human overrides, adoption, time saved and cost per interaction.

Outcome: evidence for scale, refine, hold or stop.
Scale

Consulting output: phased recommendation rather than automatic expansion.
