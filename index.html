01 · Business Discovery

Understand the operational problem before proposing a solution.

Apex Operational Systems Ltd operates a business critical fulfilment platform supporting robotic movement, inventory, order processing and warehouse operations. Discovery identified recurring operational disruption, slow investigation, fragmented knowledge, infrastructure resilience concerns and deployment risk.

Operational Stability Issues

Recurring high severity outages disrupt robotic movement, stock allocation and order picking across fulfilment operations.

Limited High Availability & DR

Manual standby infrastructure and limited automated failover increase recovery times and create single points of failure.

Reactive Support Model

Issues are usually identified after service degradation or failure, with limited proactive alerting and slow root cause investigation.

Siloed Teams & Knowledge Gaps

Development, testing and support work independently, creating poor cross-team visibility and inconsistent troubleshooting.

On-Premises Infrastructure Constraints

Database timeouts, network instability, maintenance overhead and limited horizontal scalability affect peak periods.

Deployment & Change Risk

Hotfixes and changes require stronger CI/CD controls, validation, rollback planning and post release monitoring.
