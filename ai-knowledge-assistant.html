AI Knowledge Assistant

A focused proof of concept for governed, retrieval-augmented operational support using approved enterprise knowledge, source references and human-reviewed recommendations.

Why This Phase Exists

The current operating model relies heavily on engineers manually searching logs, database records, tickets and technical documentation during incidents. The AI Knowledge Assistant is intended to reduce that search burden by helping users retrieve relevant, approved knowledge and receive grounded guidance without giving the AI direct operational authority.

Business Problem

Incident investigation is slowed by fragmented knowledge, repeated searches and dependency on individual engineer experience.

Target Outcome

Provide faster access to trusted operational knowledge while preserving human judgement, traceability and accountability.

Scope

Approved runbooks, known-error guidance, architecture notes, incident knowledge and other governed operational content.

Not in Scope

The assistant will not autonomously change production systems, execute recovery actions or make unreviewed operational decisions.

Proposed AI Workflow

The proof of concept is designed around retrieval and decision support rather than unrestricted automation.

01

Governed Sources

Approved operational documents and knowledge are selected for ingestion.

02

Ingestion

Content is prepared, indexed and made available for semantic retrieval.

03

User Question

An engineer asks an operational or troubleshooting question.

04

Grounded Response

Relevant source material is retrieved and used to generate a cited answer.

05

Human Review

The user validates the recommendation before any operational action is taken.

Planned Prototype Evidence

The first prototype will focus on demonstrating that the architectural approach is useful, controlled and explainable.

Governed Knowledge

Evidence: A small approved document set with clear ownership, scope and source provenance.

Semantic Retrieval

Evidence: Retrieval of relevant source chunks based on operational questions rather than simple keyword matching.

Grounded Answers

Evidence: Responses constrained by retrieved source material instead of unsupported free-form generation.

Source Citations

Evidence: Users can see which approved documents or references informed the generated response.

Feedback Capture

Evidence: A simple mechanism for users to indicate whether the response was useful or requires improvement.

Human Validation

Evidence: Recommendations remain advisory and require engineer review before operational decisions are made.

Architecture Decision

AI as decision support, not operational authority

The initial design deliberately keeps the assistant outside the production control path. Accuracy, explainability, source traceability and safe adoption are prioritised over full automation. This reduces operational risk while allowing the organisation to validate usefulness, quality and user trust before considering more advanced automation.

Success Criteria

Relevant retrieval

The assistant consistently retrieves source material that is useful for the operational question being asked.

Traceable answers

Generated guidance includes enough source context for users to verify where the information came from.

Useful operational support

Users can find trusted information faster than relying solely on manual document and ticket searches.

Controlled risk

The assistant cannot independently execute production actions and keeps the human operator accountable for decisions.

Feedback-driven improvement

User feedback can be captured and used to improve knowledge quality, retrieval and response usefulness.

Planned Delivery Path

The phase is intentionally small so the highest-value assumptions can be validated before increasing implementation complexity.

01

Prepare Knowledge

Select approved operational documents, define ownership and remove unsuitable or outdated content.

02

Build the Prototype

Implement ingestion, semantic retrieval, grounded generation, citations and a simple user interface.

03

Validate With Users

Test representative operational questions, capture feedback and measure answer usefulness and traceability.

What This Demonstrates

Enterprise AI

AI is introduced in response to a defined operational problem rather than as a standalone technology experiment.

RAG Architecture

The solution separates trusted knowledge retrieval from generative response creation to improve grounding and traceability.

Responsible AI

Human review, controlled scope, approved sources and feedback mechanisms are built into the operating model.

Solution Design

The phase links a business problem to requirements, architectural controls, prototype evidence and measurable validation criteria.
